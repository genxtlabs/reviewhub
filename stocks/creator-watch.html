NSE vs. BSE valuation, ahead of the NSE IPO

Where tracked creators' views overlap — presented side by side, neither ranked nor endorsed.
CA Rachana Phadke RanadeSees NSE as structurally closer to its ceiling given its already near-total (92-99%) share of cash and futures trading, versus BSE's growth coming off a much lower options-market base — and calculated NSE's FY26 PE (~43x) as slightly below BSE's (~47x) at the time.
Pranjal KamraAlso flagged BSE's rising options-market share as a real competitive risk to NSE, but concluded NSE looks fairly-to-cheaply valued versus BSE on his own like-for-like comparison — while disclosing he personally holds BSE shares, a relevant conflict of interest to weigh against his framing.
Both creators point to the same underlying fact (BSE's rapid options-share gain since 2023) but land on different relative-value conclusions, partly using different PE comparisons calculated at different points in time. BSE vs NSE: Can NSE Repeat BSE's Massive Growth Story?
