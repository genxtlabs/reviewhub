The Verdict

Reviewers are near-unanimous that Nadhi Kadhalan is one of the strongest Tamil releases of the year, built around a career-best performance from its lead and cinematography that makes the river setting a character in its own right. The debut composer’s score draws consistent praise. The main hesitation across reviews is pacing — several critics flag a slow opening thirty minutes — though most agree the patient build pays off by the film’s quiet, understated ending.
